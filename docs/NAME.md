# Why the name is Grey Conseil

**Grey Eminence** (Matt’s app) and **Grey Conseil** (this unofficial fork) are two jokes in the same family. One is the power behind the throne. The other is the valet who comes along, keeps the notes, and offers counsel — if monsieur pleases.

This is **not** Matt’s product. The name is homage, plus preferred humor. It is not a claim to be Grey Eminence.

## Grey Eminence ← *éminence grise*

English **grey eminence** is a calque of French ***éminence grise***: someone who exercises power and influence **without holding the official position**.

The original *éminence grise* was **François Leclerc du Tremblay** (1577–1638), the Capuchin known as **Père Joseph**. He was Cardinal Richelieu’s confidential agent. Richelieu, as Louis XIII’s chief minister, practically ran France; Père Joseph ran a great deal of it from beside him.

- Cardinals were styled ***Éminence***. Richelieu’s red dress made him ***l’Éminence rouge***.
- Père Joseph’s **grey** Capuchin habit made him ***l’Éminence grise*** — distinguished from Richelieu, and associated with him.

A short history of the phrase, with the 1718 epitaph and the first English uses: [wordhistories.net — meaning and origin of ‘éminence grise’](https://wordhistories.net/2019/07/24/eminence-grise/).

Matt named the meeting notebook for that figure: the quiet counselor who is not on the letterhead.

## Grey Conseil ← *éminence grise* + Jules Verne

**Grey** is the same colour as *grise* — the unofficial counselor, not a second Grey Eminence. **Conseil** is French for *counsel*, *advice*, *council* — and it is a character.

In Jules Verne’s ***Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea*** (*Vingt mille lieues sous les mers*), **Conseil** is Professor Aronnax’s servant: loyal, unflappable, classifying every fish in Linnaean Latin while the *Nautilus* goes wherever Nemo takes it. He is not the captain. He is the man who comes along, keeps the catalogue, and says (in the usual Englishing) that he is of monsieur’s opinion.

You will not get the joke from a one-line summary. Read the book: [archive.org — *20000 Leagues Under The Sea* (Verne)](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.459144).

So:

| | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Grey Eminence** | The grey counselor behind the throne (Père Joseph). Matt’s name. |
| **Grey** | Still that grey — the unofficial counselor, not the red cardinal. |
| **Conseil** | Counsel, and Verne’s servant who files the notes on the voyage. |

**Grey Conseil** is the fork that sits beside Grey Eminence, takes the minutes, and does not pretend to be the ministry.

## What the name is not

- Not a rebrand of Matt’s shipping app.
- Not a company name. The Dock title is **Grey Conseil**. `ftlN` on the version string is a test-build counter, not a brand.
- Not a claim that this build is the *éminence grise* of anyone’s company. That would miss the joke.
